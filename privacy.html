Privacy information

Do not enter confidential or sensitive personal information into the AI chat. It is not a privileged channel for speaking with an attorney.

AI questions

When you submit a question, your question, selected jurisdiction, and up to four recent messages in that browser session are sent to this website's Cloudflare backend and the configured AI provider (Cloudflare Workers AI by default, or OpenAI if the operator enables it). The website does not intentionally create a database of chat transcripts. Cloudflare and the AI provider may process request information under their own applicable terms, security controls, and retention policies. Network and security logs may contain request metadata. The site owner should review current vendor data-processing terms before launching to the public.

Document formatting

The pleading-paper editor processes the text you type or paste within your browser. Its preview, Word download, and print/PDF flows do not send document content to the AI backend. Closing or reloading the page clears this editor's unsaved contents. If you choose to print or save a file, your browser, operating system, or installed software handles that file.

Verification, analytics, and advertising

If anti-bot verification is enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile may process information necessary to detect automated requests. Standard hosting security logs may collect information such as IP address, user agent, and request time. This starter website does not currently install advertising scripts, a tracking cookie, or an analytics service. If advertising or analytics is introduced, this notice should be updated before activation and any legally required consent mechanism implemented.

Contact and operator details

Before public launch, the site owner should add a working contact email and the responsible operator's business or legal identity to this notice. Until then, do not submit sensitive information through the AI chat.

This is a starter privacy notice, not a jurisdiction-specific privacy compliance certification. A site owner should review it for the actual deployment, AI provider, advertising arrangements, and relevant privacy laws.
