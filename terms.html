Terms & legal disclaimer

American Legal Assistance is an independent general-information tool, not a law firm, lawyer referral service, or substitute for advice from a licensed attorney. No attorney-client relationship or attorney-client privilege is created by visiting the site, submitting questions, or generating a file.

Accuracy and time-sensitive information

AI answers may contain errors, omissions, outdated rules, incorrect citations, or inapplicable information. The AI does not automatically check live statutes, court dockets, local court instructions, or deadlines. Verify information with current official sources and obtain qualified legal advice before acting. Do not rely on AI answers to calculate a filing or response deadline.

Documents

The pleading-paper tool is a general formatting utility for user-supplied text. Its output is not reviewed by an attorney and is not guaranteed to meet court rules, be legally sufficient, or be accepted for filing. Many proceedings require specific official forms, signatures, accompanying documents, proof of service, or special formatting. The website does not electronically file documents or send them to courts.

Use and emergencies

Do not enter Social Security numbers, financial credentials, minors' identifying details, protected health information, or confidential case materials into the AI chat. For emergencies, contact emergency services. If you have a time-sensitive legal issue, seek prompt help from a licensed attorney or the appropriate court self-help center.

Site operator and contact

Before inviting the public, the website operator should publish an appropriate contact address, their legal/business identity, and any required jurisdiction-specific disclosures.
