California pleading paper

Begin with a blank numbered sheet, or enter case information and add structured headings and paragraphs. Preview and download an editable Word document, or print/save it as a PDF. Your text stays in this browser unless you choose to send a separate question to AI.

Document sections

Build the document one section at a time. Choose a heading, paragraph, or blank line; these stay distinct in Word and PDF.

To make a blank sheet, leave case information and sections empty. To build a document, enter case details above, then add headings and paragraphs here. Headings are not inferred from plain text.

This tool formats text and section headings; it does not produce court-approved forms, check legal sufficiency, calculate filing deadlines, or file anything. Review the court's current rules before using a document. Official California court forms and templates ↗
