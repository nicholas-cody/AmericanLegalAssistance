About American Legal Assistance

American Legal Assistance is an independent website designed to make general U.S. legal information easier to understand. Its simple AI prompt helps visitors learn basic concepts and locate official self-help information. Its California pleading-paper editor helps visitors format their own text.

Our Five-Point Guide

After an AI answer is returned, the ALA Five-Point Guide can make a second, focused AI pass over that existing answer and reorganize it into: what the issue generally involves, what information may be useful to organize, what should be verified with an official source, categories of options already discussed, how a document workspace may help, and one non-directive possible next step. The guide is designed to make the answer easier to act on without turning it into individualized legal advice.

We are not a law firm. The AI is not a lawyer, and use of this website does not establish an attorney-client relationship or create attorney-client privilege. The AI may be inaccurate or out of date and cannot review your case or check current court rules. This service does not provide legal representation, individualized legal advice, court filing, or guaranteed court-ready documents.

Where to get personal help

For advice about your specific situation, a deadline, a court filing, or your legal rights and obligations, consult an attorney licensed in the relevant jurisdiction or your court's self-help center. Visit our free legal resources page for official sources and legal-aid directories.

Advertising

We may display clearly labeled advertising on public informational pages in the future. Advertising does not determine the AI's answers. This website does not currently contain an activated advertising network.

ALA Private Session

American Legal Assistance is designed so visitors can ask a general legal-information question without creating an AI account or leaving the site. AI questions are processed through our Cloudflare backend; ALA does not keep a prompt-history database. Pleading-paper drafts autosave only in the visitor’s own browser.
