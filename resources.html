Free legal resources

You do not need to pay for access to official court forms and many court self-help resources. These links take you to third-party government or nonprofit websites. Check the page's date and your own court's instructions.

California courts

  • California Courts Self-Help Guide — explanations of common civil, family, housing, and other court procedures.
  • Find and fill out California court forms — official forms and a Word pleading-paper template.
  • California Rules of Court — review statewide rules, including formatting rules 2.100–2.111.
  • Find your California court — your local court may have additional instructions and self-help information.

United States

  • Legal Services Corporation: find legal aid — locate nonprofit legal-aid services in your area.
  • USA.gov: free and low-cost legal help — federal government information about legal assistance.
  • U.S. Courts: federal court forms — official forms for federal courts.

About pleading paper

California trial court filings can have requirements for margins, numbered lines, signatures, and the first page. Many matters require a Judicial Council form instead of a custom pleading. Use the pleading-paper formatter for your own text only after checking that a pleading paper filing is appropriate and that your court accepts the document format.
